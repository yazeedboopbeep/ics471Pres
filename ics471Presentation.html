    After the product ships, the model no longer has access to labeled data.

    What’s the fix?

    In the lab, labels drive every update

    After shipping, the live data drives the update

    Test-time adaptation: recompute BatchNorm statistics on each live batch, then take a gradient step on γ and β with a loss that needs no labels.

    BatchNorm was built for shifting weights

    Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015

    TTA reuses it for shifting environments

    Wang et al., 2021

    Internal covariate shift comes from updated weights; covariate shift comes from updated environments. BatchNorm handles both when its statistics are current.
